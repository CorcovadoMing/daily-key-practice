## Bundled iOS Safari metronome gain

- iPhone/iPad Safari (including desktop-mode iPadOS) uses peak 0.85 instead of 0.65, approximately +2.3 dB. This is a modest digital adjustment with 15% headroom, not a guarantee of equal perceived loudness across devices or outputs.
- Desktop, Android, and recognized alternative iOS browsers retain 0.65. Existing volume slider and zero-volume silence remain available, especially for headphone use.
- Worklet and fallback use the same peak; waveform, accent frequencies, envelope durations and beat scheduling are unchanged. Worklet asset URL is cache-busted to this release.
- Synthetic tests render 44.1/48 kHz at 30/80/240 BPM, check bounded samples, silence and equal beat timing; controller tests verify fallback gain ramps and platform routing. No physical iPhone/iPad or headphone listening test was possible here. Device listening validation remains necessary.
